# Documentation for Users

Katalyst Content provides a block-based editor for composing page content from
items — text, images, tables, and layout sections — arranged in a nested tree.

## States

**Unpublished** — the content has never been published. You can add and
arrange items and save your work, but visitors can't see anything yet.

**Draft** — there are saved changes that haven't been published. Visitors see
the previously published version; the status bar links to a preview of your
draft.

**Published** — the saved content is live. Visitors see exactly what the
editor shows.

## Saving and publishing

**Save** stores your changes as a draft without affecting the published
version. **Publish** makes the current draft live. **Revert** discards the
saved draft and returns to the published version. **Discard** abandons unsaved
changes in the editor.

## Item actions

**Collapse / Expand** — hides or shows an item's nested children in the
editor, to make large trees easier to work with. Collapsing doesn't change
the content.

**Indent / Outdent** — moves an item deeper into or out of the tree, nesting
it under the item above or promoting it a level.

**Reorder** — drag an item by its row to move it. Nested children move with
their parent.

**Edit** — opens the item's form to change its content and settings.

**Remove** — deletes the item (and its nested children) from the draft. The
published version is unaffected until you publish.

**Hidden** — items can be marked not visible in their settings; they stay in
the tree, marked with a hidden icon, but are not shown to visitors.
